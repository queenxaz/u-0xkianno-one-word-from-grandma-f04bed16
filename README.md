One word, as if from a grandma: the word she would text about AI.

## The word

**Alexa**

Ask a grandma what AI is and she will not give you a definition. She will
name the one machine she actually talks to: the little speaker in her
kitchen. "Alexa" is warm, a little baffled, and funny all on its own — no
caption, no explanation, just the name of the robot she already bosses
around. It is tied to machines, instantly readable, and unmistakably a
grandma's text.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no
randomness, no arguments — the word is fixed. It creates `/output` if it is
missing and never deletes it.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
